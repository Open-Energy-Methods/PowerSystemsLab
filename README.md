# PowerSystemsLab

An independent computational and experimental learning programme in electrical power systems engineering, maintained under Open Energy Methods.

**Status:** Foundation stage. No qualified simulation results or experimental claims yet.

The programme develops skills from first principles through reproducible benchmarks, industrial software studies and integrated engineering applications. MissionCriticalGrid, a synthetic data-centre electrical infrastructure study, is the first flagship application. Progress is competence-based, not deadline-based.

Models are educational and research-oriented, not certified designs or substitutes for professional engineering review. Commercial software assets and results are subject to their respective licences.
