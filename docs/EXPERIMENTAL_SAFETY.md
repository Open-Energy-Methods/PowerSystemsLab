# Experimental safety

All physical work is planned only, not authorised by this document.

Initial experiments are restricted to isolated, current-limited extra-low-voltage educational supplies and safe signal-level measurement. Define energy limits, fusing, isolation, emergency stop, enclosure, thermal limits and risk assessment with the responsible lab supervisor before assembly or energisation.

No mains-connected, high-voltage, high-current fault, grid-connected converter or exposed energy-storage experiments in an unsupervised workshop. Such work requires an appropriately equipped electrical lab, competent supervision and its documented approval. Avoid unsafe battery packs, rotating shafts without guarding, and earth-referenced oscilloscope connection to live circuits. Do not defeat interlocks or protective earthing.

Before each test: approved method, equipment ratings, isolation and grounding plan, calibration, safe shutdown, hazard review, designated supervisor when required and acceptance criteria. Record incident/near-miss and stop if conditions differ from the plan.

Candidate safe investigations: isolated low-energy RLC phasors; guarded small machine bench; low-voltage current-limited converter; signal-level simulated relay logic. These are subject to approval and are not build instructions.

Validation requires calibration, measurement uncertainty, repeatability and documented operating conditions. Simulation results never establish safe hardware operation.
