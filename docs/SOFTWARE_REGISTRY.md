## Access gates
Do not activate limited evaluations until input cases and comparison metrics are prepared. No bypassing licence limits. Obtain explicit publication permission where unclear, including numerical results and screenshots; keep restricted materials outside the public working tree. Never claim a feature or model is qualified from a vendor feature list. Record supported fault types and current definitions; pandapower near-generator peak/thermal duties are a known version-specific qualification question.
