## Critical assessment and project implications

The accepted manuscript excludes microgrid-specific stability, purely local control instabilities, distribution impacts covered elsewhere, and wave propagation. Do not claim it validates islanded generator performance. Use the five-category taxonomy to choose RMS/EMT and eigenvalue methods by phenomenon rather than tool name; separate small-signal and large-disturbance studies. SCR thresholds are examples conditional on controls and system configuration, not universal acceptance criteria.
