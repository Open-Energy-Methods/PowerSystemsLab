## Critical assessment and project implications

The source is a TSO perspective, not a site design manual. Fig.5 and pp.8–9 support separate IT and cooling paths, not our numerical demand assumptions. The report describes UPS-less exceptions, so declare a traditional UPS-backed availability-oriented campus as the reference and keep AI-training variants separate. Distinguish current operating risks, planning adequacy and conditional flexibility; no universal FRT or PUE requirements derived from this report.
